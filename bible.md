# SACRO — Master Project Bible
Version 2.0 · July 2026 · Status: Canon

## Core Identity

**Artist name:** Sacro
**Mission:** Create work that lasts. Music is a vehicle to explore identity, truth, memory, faith, transformation, Puerto Rico, and becoming. The goal is not to make good songs — it is to build a world. Every release makes the universe larger.

**Long-term goal:** A catalog that feels interconnected — recurring places, symbols, sounds, and characters — that rewards people who stay for years. Someone can begin with the newest release, go back years later, and discover meanings they never noticed. The goal is not to impress. The goal is to endure.

## Production Philosophy

**The most important rule: production behaves like the camera.**

- Confusion → blurry production
- Driving → moving stereo field
- Studio → intimate, prophetic, focused
- Transformation → arrangements expand
- Memory → tape textures, room tone, piano, vinyl noise
- Acceptance → silence, air, restraint

Songs are not isolated. The whole body of work is one film score with songs inside.

## Visual Philosophy

Puerto Rico. Warm, humid, wind, night. Analog, dream logic, real locations, minimal CGI. Cinematic, patient, slow cinema, natural performances, warm light, roads, ocean.

## The Prequel

Explains emotionally — not literally — how the protagonist arrives at *Del Amor al Arte*. It is about noise, loss, finding purpose, returning home, accepting identity. It prepares the audience emotionally; it does not explain every event.

### Otra Botella
Noise, distraction, party culture, searching, conflict. **Video:** large party, good energy, Paula present. As the night progresses they fight; the room gets louder — "Trato de escuchar pero es que el ruido no me deja" becomes literal. **Ending:** driving home, silence, Paula upset, bright white light, cut. No explanation.

### Bocanada
Begins with white light. Dream logic, forest, nature, time feels strange. Paula present throughout but becomes emotionally unreachable. Near the end they stand together; she quietly walks away. Sacro does not chase — the camera stays with him. **This is the emotional turning point: she stops being physically present and becomes memory.**

### Life
Birth of the artist. Inspired by real life — after the relationship changed in 2023, music became purpose. **Video:** live performance (possibly Segunda Posita). The audience meets Sacro as an artist for the first time. NOT about winning Paula back — about discovering purpose.

### Simplemente No Hay Conexión
Return home, roots, acceptance, preparation. **Video:** walking across rocks toward Primera Posita; sitting on the beach with headphones and notebook, writing, watching the ocean. Bike ride through Primera Posita — neighbors, kids, life continues. Second verse: return home ("Ya no tengo espacio para respirar..."), enter house ("Toy en el proceso..."), put on the black suit ("Hasta que Dios me guarde..."), mirror. Last chorus: coffee, grab belongings, leave the house. **Ends immediately before the beginning of Del Amor al Arte.**

## Del Amor al Arte

**Main thesis: NOT a breakup album.** It is about identity, truth, transformation, memory, time, faith, hope, destiny, purpose, art. Love is the vehicle. Art is the destination.

### Film Structure

- **Act I — Sample SB, Guaraguau, 2027:** Awakening. Journey. Future.
- **Act II — Sitcom, Actos, Ricky Pista 2:** Reality breaks. Transformation. Search.
- **Act III — Como Si Na', Hambre, Cerámica:** Perspective. Ceremony. Acceptance.

## Important Lessons

Do not force songs to become longer. Short songs are okay if they behave like scenes. The project behaves better as a film than as a playlist.

## Strengths & Risks

**Strengths:** world building, symbolism, consistency, long-term thinking, honesty, willingness to revise.
**Risks:** overexplaining, adding ideas because they sound cool, losing emotional simplicity, trying to answer every mystery.

## Real-Life Context

2023: relationship changed; music became central; started performing live. This emotional truth inspires the films. The films are inspired by reality — they are not documentaries.

## Working Style

Sacro responds best to honest critique, big-picture thinking, film references, and structure. No empty praise — decisions. Whenever discussing the project, always ask: **does this serve the film, or only the scene?**
